Default Policy

What happens when things go wrong.

We've had creators not deliver. We're putting our policy in writing so you know exactly where you stand — before you sign.

If a Creator Doesn't Deliver
BrandSetu will provide a replacement creator of the same niche and tier within 7 days.

If a suitable replacement cannot be sourced, the management fee for that creator (₹500) will be refunded.

The product sent to the defaulting creator cannot be recovered or refunded — it stays with the creator. This is why we carefully vet every creator before onboarding.
If the Brand Delays Product Dispatch
Creator's 7-day shoot window starts from the day the product is received — not from campaign start.

If dispatch is delayed by more than 7 days without notice, BrandSetu reserves the right to reschedule or reassign the campaign slot.
No Refund Cases
Content was created but brand dislikes it — creative differences are not grounds for refund. Brief must be clear upfront.

Views/reach didn't meet expectations — we never promised views. Organic reach is unpredictable by design.

Campaign fully executed — once video is live and verified, deal is complete.
⚠️

In our first two campaigns — Qalb (15 creators) and OneGummy (40 creators) — 5 creators did not deliver. We replaced where possible and own that fully. That's why this policy now exists in writing.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Real questions, real answers — no marketing fluff.

Can I approve the creator before they're confirmed?
Yes. We share creator profiles — niche, follower count, sample content — before confirming. You can approve or request alternatives. Final list is locked only after your sign-off.
What if the product gets lost in transit?
Transit responsibility lies with the brand. We recommend tracking-enabled shipping. If a product is lost, brand must reship — the 7-day creator window resets from new delivery. BrandSetu will follow up with creator.
Can I request specific content formats?
For paid collabs, yes — detailed briefs are shared with creators. For barter, you can provide reference content and key talking points, but we don't enforce scripts. Authentic content performs better.
What if I'm unhappy with the content quality?
Content quality feedback must be raised within 48 hours of receiving the draft (for paid collabs with review rounds). For barter, creators own their creative — however, clear brief = better results. Quality concerns before campaigns start, not after.
Do you work with nano creators (under 10K)?
Yes — our network includes micro and nano creators in specific niches who deliver strong engagement rates. Follower count alone doesn't determine fit. We match on niche relevance and content quality.
How do I pay BrandSetu?
Via UPI, bank transfer, or razorpay link — whatever is convenient. Invoice shared before campaign begins. No payment, no campaign start. Simple.
