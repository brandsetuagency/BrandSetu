Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you apply as a creator or send a brand enquiry, we collect the details you enter: name, phone or WhatsApp number, Instagram handle, follower count, niche, collaboration preferences, and any message you write. Creators who use our onboarding form may also share email, city, audience details and rates.

Why we collect it

  • To match creators with relevant brand collaborations
  • To contact you about campaigns and applications
  • To run and improve BrandSetu

Who sees it

Your details are visible to the BrandSetu team. Creator profile details (name, Instagram handle, niche, follower range, rates where relevant) may be shared with brands considering you for a campaign. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase. Public sections of our site show only information we have chosen to publish, such as approved reviews, creator names with Instagram links, and brand partners.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or remove your profile from our website, by writing to brandsetu.connect@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp.

Terms of Collaboration

Barter collaborations

  • The brand sends a PR package with 2 to 4 product options to each creator.
  • Each creator delivers 1 Reel and 1 Story. Creators keep the products they receive.
  • Creators shoot within 1 week of receiving the product. The brand receives the video within 10 days of product delivery.
  • The brand shares the intended upload timeline at least 1 week in advance.

Paid collaborations

  • Rates are agreed between the brand and the creator before work begins.
  • Creator payments are released 15 to 30 days after the video goes live.

General

  • BrandSetu’s management fee is quoted to the brand before a campaign starts.
  • We do not guarantee views, reach or sales. Creators produce content in their own style.
  • Running paid ads on creator content requires separate ads rights, agreed with each creator.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee a collaboration. Selection depends on campaign needs and audience fit.
